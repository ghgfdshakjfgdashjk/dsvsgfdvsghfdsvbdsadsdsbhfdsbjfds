Run a script of clicks and keystrokes instead of one plain click.

Braced tokens are buttons, keys and timing; everything else is typed literally. The whole script counts as one repetition, so your CPS becomes repetitions per second. A wait inside it adds to the delay rather than fitting inside it, so a ten millisecond wait at a ten millisecond delay repeats every twenty milliseconds.

Clicks: {LMB} {RMB} {MMB} {MB4} {MB5}
Hold and release: add D or U — {LMBD} {LMBU} {RMBD} …
Keys: {SPACE} {ENTER} {TAB} {SHIFT} {CTRL} {ALT} {F1}–{F24}, or {KEYDOWN:SHIFT} {KEYUP:SHIFT}
Timing: {WAITMS:10} {WAITS:1.5} · Wheel: {SCROLL:-3}

Any script containing a wait is played step by step rather than batched, so it can't reach five-figure rates.
